# Floorwise

People describe a place; the building computes a route and shows what a change would do before it is applied.

Primary users: a visitor, a wheelchair user, a technician carrying a load, and a facility manager. The demo follows a campus visitor, previews an eye-level route, closes a lift, then simulates a verified door-width repair.

Success means grounded answers, a visible interpretation, a followable route, explicit limits, and recoverable failures. Claude handles language; deterministic code owns every number and path. Benchmark costs, walking distance, and exit-rooted impact are separate quantities.

The demo is schematic and manually anchored. It does not track people, certify accessibility, or perform building work. Missing geometry is disclosed. No IFC adapter, AR, free roaming, or photorealism in this iteration.
