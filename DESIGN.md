# Design

Cinematic industrial: a dark architectural stage, softly illuminated floor plates, crisp white structural edges, amber route, and sparse contextual labels. The building dominates; the conversation is a compact operational panel. No glass cards, gradients, ring gauges, decorative dashboards, or automatic camera tours.

Use Barlow with a strong size hierarchy. Stage #10171c, panels #182329, text #edf3f5, muted text #b6c5cc, route #ffd45c, blocked #ff8a8f, accessible #85dfb2. Pair every status colour with words and an icon. Preserve a bright 2D floor-plan fallback. On a projector, require 4.5:1 text/route contrast; use the light plan mode if ambient conditions defeat the dark model.

Modes: Building, Floor plan, Follow route. User actions initiate camera changes. Route playback has no head bob; connector interiors without data use a labeled transition. Reduced motion uses static waypoints. Keyboard and touch controls remain visible. Every surface has loading, error, empty and no-route states.

Inspect at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Avoid labels that compete with the route. Demo facts and model assumptions remain inspectable.
